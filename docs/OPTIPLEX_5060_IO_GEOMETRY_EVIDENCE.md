# OptiPlex 5060 Tower — external I/O evidence audit

Dell's Ports and Connectors specification and its front/rear text legends document port identity and standard counts. This is a platform-family reference; it does not select a retail SKU, Service Tag, motherboard revision or optional-module population.

**Image discrepancy:** the current Dell front- and rear-view pages are under the OptiPlex 5060 guide URL, but both embedded illustrations visibly say “OptiPlex 5070”. Their pixel geometry, proportions and coordinates were excluded from the 5060 reconstruction. Text legends are used only for identity, counts and callout order. 3D I/O placement is schematic.

| Region | Standard count | Contents |
|---|---:|---|
| Front | 5 | One headset/audio jack and four USB ports |
| Rear data/audio/network | 10 | One line-out, two DisplayPorts, four USB 3.1 Gen 1, two USB 2.0 SmartPower On and one RJ-45 |
| Rear power | 1 | PSU AC inlet, counted separately |

Optional SD reader, optical drive, serial module, third-video module and external antenna connectors are not counted as installed. HDMI 2.0b, DisplayPort, VGA and USB-C DP Alt Mode are alternatives for the optional third-video module, not simultaneous outputs.

The model now has distinct selectable proxy meshes for standard front ports/indicators, rear ports, expansion openings, PSU inlet, diagnostic LED and chassis lock features. Port type/count is source-backed; aperture dimensions, spacing, panel coordinates and optional-module population remain unknown. Chassis folds, carrier rails and port meshes are illustrative, not CAD. The vent-grille primitive is implemented and tested but not attached to the 5060 model without a matching source.
