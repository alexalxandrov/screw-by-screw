## Accuracy and service boundary

The pilot remains `illustrative_proxy_not_cad`. Official 201.8 × 110 mm dimensions describe the nominal card; the reviewer-observed shroud overhang is not quantified and the nominal length is not treated as a maximum outer-shroud bound. Fan opening/impeller diameters are approximate reviewer measurements. Eight blade surfaces per fan are photo-counted; the low-relief E-like blade marks, hub badge and simplified EVGA / GEFORCE RTX shroud wordmarks are visual proxies, not exact logo or OEM geometry. Blade profile, pitch, camber and original fan identity remain unverified. The 24 fin meshes, six heatpipe run proxies, PCB aperture, component locations and other mesh dimensions are educational geometry, not metrology.

The heatpipe sources use different counting conventions. Six selectable run proxies represent four central GPU-contact runs and two side runs described by one exact-SKU review; they do not establish six separate closed heatpipe loops. Other sources describe four copper heatpipes or two central tube paths. The physical loop count and exact routing remain unresolved.

Five Gamers Nexus partial teardown observations are represented as separate observations: four spring-loaded GPU cooler screws; a separate pair at the fin-stack base; two I/O-bracket screws with reverse-side nuts plus three display-output retaining screws; disconnecting both fan leads; and three retaining screws per fan. Tom's Hardware separately reports an eight-screw cooler/shroud aggregate tied to a review sample labelled with the exact retail SKU. Its overlap with the Gamers Nexus groups is unknown, so counts are not added. Additional shroud fasteners remain an uncertain 4-or-6 candidate. None of this constitutes a complete disassembly/reassembly guide.

The six Samsung GDDR6 package identities are exact-SKU-linked through teardown/review material, but their 3D centres are photo-estimated. Device-level density, organisation and package details are attributed to a secondary catalog, not a primary Samsung datasheet. Six logical memory-interface presentation groups are not mapped one-to-one to the six packages.

The power layer includes the teardown-observed auxiliary-input shunt (5 mΩ), a separate PCIe-slot shunt whose resistance remains unknown, one observed fuse with unknown rating, two inductor packages in an area tentatively described as memory VRM, and an observed MOSFET/contact-plate region. It is not a PCB schematic. Exact PCB revision, applicable BoardView/schematic, BOM, VRM phase topology, component identities/values, electrical nets and connector pinouts remain unknown.

The two authored animations are educational presentation. Rotor motion is not measured RPM. The exploded overview's offsets are not physical service clearances or a removal order. The ruler reports loaded-model-space distances only.

## 1.0 release rule

Do not tag 1.0 while any owner-acceptance gate remains unchecked. An educational release may ship only with clear proxy labels and no implied repair, manufacturing or fitment accuracy. A precision engineering release requires measured geometry, exact PCB revision identification, cleared assets and separate engineering validation.

The exact-SKU-linked teardown sample also has two reported unused memory locations alongside six fitted GDDR6 packages. They are represented as two separate logical-only PCB nodes, not as additional memory channels or an upgrade path.
