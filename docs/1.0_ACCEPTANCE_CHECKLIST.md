## Accuracy boundary

The pilot model remains `illustrative_proxy_not_cad`. It contains separate fan, heatsink-fin, heatpipe, PCB, GPU, memory and power-stage proxies. Generated scene bounds are structural measurements of proxy geometry, not measurements of the physical card. The published 201.8 mm length is nominal and is not treated as a maximum outer-shroud extent; the reported shroud overhang is not quantified.

The six Samsung GDDR6 packages are supported by exact-model teardown imagery, but their 3D coordinates are photo-estimated. The exact PCB revision, full native CAD, exact BoardView/schematic, complete BOM, verified electrical routing and metrology-grade geometry remain unavailable or unverified.

The power and cooling layers are qualitative educational explanations. They are not CFD, thermal FEA, SPICE, electrical net tracing, measured fan RPM or a service procedure. The ruler measures the loaded model only. The assembly panel deliberately leaves the procedural sequence empty until the full exact-model teardown is reviewed.

## 1.0 release rule

Do not tag 1.0 while any owner-acceptance gate remains unchecked. An educational release with clearly labelled proxy geometry may be considered only when the intended scope is explicitly educational and no repair, manufacturing or fitment accuracy is implied. A precision engineering release requires measured geometry, exact PCB revision identification, cleared assets and separate engineering validation.
