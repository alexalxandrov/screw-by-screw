## Accuracy boundary

The pilot model remains `illustrative_proxy_not_cad`. It contains 24 fin proxies, fan blade proxies, an illustrative PCB outline, internal component proxies and an approximate shroud contour. The CI scene bounds are structural measurements of generated proxy geometry, not measurements of the physical card. The published 201.8 mm length is nominal and is not a hard upper bound for all exterior geometry; LanOC reports an unquantified shroud overhang.

The power and cooling layers are qualitative educational explanations. They are not CFD, thermal FEA, SPICE, electrical net tracing or a service procedure. Assembly steps are intentionally not authored until the full teardown sequence is reviewed.

## 1.0 release rule

Do not tag 1.0 while any unchecked product gate remains. An educational 1.0 with clearly labelled proxy geometry is possible only if the intended scope is explicitly educational and no repair/manufacturing accuracy is implied. A precision engineering release requires measured geometry, exact PCB revision identification and separate engineering validation.
